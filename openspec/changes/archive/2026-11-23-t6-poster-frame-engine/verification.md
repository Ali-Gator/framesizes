# Verification: poster sizes and the BL-17 pairings

Checked 2026-11-23 by web search (Google US results, retailer and printer pages). Counts are what the searches showed; prices and stock move, sizes do not.

## US poster sizes

Each size must be sold as a poster size by two or more US printers or retailers.

| ----- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----- |

12x18 and 20x30 are on fewer lists than the others (FedEx's standard list skips them), but both are sold as posters at Walmart and listed by a print shop, so they pass. The SEO guide's draft range, "11x17 to 27x40", holds.

## A-series sizes (ISO 216)

A3 297 × 420 mm, A2 420 × 594 mm, A1 594 × 841 mm: ISO 216, as listed by Markus Kuhn's ISO paper sizes page (https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/~mgk25/iso-paper.html) and papersizes.org (https://www.papersizes.org/a-paper-sizes.htm). Holds.

## A2 and A1 frames sold under those names in the US (canvas Q6 A)

- A2: Walmart, Americanflat 16.5" x 23.4" poster frame (https://www.walmart.com/ip/140418549); Target, PosterPalooza A2 frame, 16.5 × 23.5 in (https://www.target.com/p/-/A-1009629026). UK shops sell many more (eframe.co.uk).
- A1: Walmart, "A1 Frame … 23.5x33.25" (https://www.walmart.com/ip/520720003) and an "A1 poster frame" category page (https://www.walmart.com/c/kp/a1-poster-frame).

Holds: US shoppers can buy a frame named A2 or A1, so "Use an A2 frame" (Q6 A) stands. No Amazon US listing came up in these searches.

## BL-17: the 20x30 and 27x40 mat pairings

- **20x30 with a 16x24 mat:** a search for exactly this pairing returned many Walmart listings, almost all from one maker (Craig Frames, "20x30 inch Picture Frame Matted for a 16x24 Photo", e.g. https://www.walmart.com/ip/328567146). They exist, but a search for the pairing cannot show what share of all 20x30 frames carry it, and one maker's range is not a market default.
- **27x40 with a 24x36 mat:** one complete Walmart listing (https://www.walmart.com/ip/17082718133, a 27x40 frame with a mat, sold as a 30 × 43 in frame); otherwise 24x36 mats for 27x40 frames are sold on their own (PosterPalooza at Target), and 28x40 frames matted for 24x36 are more common.

Outcome: no pairing change. The t2 finding (about one listing in seven) still describes the market; BL-17 stays deferred with this check's date. F6 is not affected: a 24x36 poster still lists the 27x40 with a mat (1¾ / 2¼ in), only not marked pre-cut.
